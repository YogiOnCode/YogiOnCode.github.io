02 · The story

How I got here

Hardware first. I studied Electronics and Instrumentation in Coimbatore, India, and spent my first year in industry on a non-contact thermal screener built during COVID. I have liked hardware and software together ever since.

Europe and autonomous systems. An EIT Digital dual master's took me through Budapest, Rennes and Trento, with a minor in Innovation and Entrepreneurship, where my team won Best Entrepreneurial Team. At Volvo Group and Chalmers I built the backend that turns raw routes into a road model for truck energy simulation.

Owning systems in production. At Machinify in Budapest I designed and shipped two inspection systems from scratch, then installed them on the factory floor and trained the operators who run them. At hello again in Linz I build the plugin that reads receipts from any cash register, and I have owned our team's OKRs three quarters in a row.
